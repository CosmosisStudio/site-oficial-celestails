The story

Feywood is a small survival game set in the Fantazia universe, in the days before a much greater adventure begins.

Following her banishment, the Owl — a cursed girl nobody wanted to keep — is left at the edge of the treacherous forest with nothing. To see morning, she has to hunt for food, gather materials for a fire, and stay clear of whatever else is moving between the trees.

Are you up to the challenge?

What you'll be doing

Hunt for food

Track and take down what you can. Hunger doesn't pause while you decide.

Gather materials

Wood, tinder, and whatever the forest floor gives up — you'll need all of it before dark.

Keep the fire alive

The fireplace is warmth, light, and the one thing standing between you and the treeline.

Avoid what hunts you

Dangerous creatures share the forest. Most of them are better at this than you are.

A cursed protagonist

The Owl carries something she didn't ask for — and it shapes how the forest reacts to her.

Pixel-art atmosphere

Hand-made pixel environments, heavy on mood and quiet dread.

Release
