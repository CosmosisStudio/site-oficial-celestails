The classic arcade challenge, completely reimagined.

🚀 Download on Google Play

About the Game

Prepare for an explosive, fast-paced experience where retro gameplay meets modern power-ups and epic boss battles! Brick Breaker: Rebuilt takes the timeless brick-smashing formula and turns it into a high-octane spectacle of neon, destruction, and strategy.

🔥 Key Features

  • Epic Boss Encounters: When the screen shakes and the sirens roar, get ready! Face off against massive bosses with unique attack patterns in high-stakes, heart-pounding battles.
  • Devastating Abilities: Harness the power of the Black Hole, activate the Shield for ultimate defense, or infect bricks with Poison. Master your cooldowns to clear the screen in seconds!
  • Deep Upgrade System: Invest your earnings into your arsenal. Over 10 upgrades to unlock and max out.
  • Smooth Performance: Optimized specifically for mobile. Experience hundreds of balls on screen at once with zero lag and rock-solid 60 FPS action.
  • Offline Mastery: No internet? No problem. Break bricks anytime, anywhere—perfect for quick sessions or long marathons.

🕹️ How to Play

  • Slide & Aim: Responsive touch controls make aiming intuitive and precise.
  • Activate Skills: Tap your unlocked abilities at the right moment to turn the tide of battle.
  • Survive the Chaos: Manage the bounce, master the angles, and survive the warning sirens to become the ultimate rebuilder!
