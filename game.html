About the game

Brick Breaker: Rebuilt takes the timeless brick-smashing formula and turns it into a fast, loud, neon spectacle. Retro gameplay, modern power-ups, and boss fights that actually fight back.

Every run is a mix of aim, timing, and knowing when to burn an ability. Clear the board fast enough and the multipliers do the rest.

Key features

Epic boss encounters

Massive bosses with their own attack patterns, each one a real wall to get past.

Devastating abilities

Black holes, shields, and poison that can clear a packed board in seconds.

Deep upgrade system

Spend your in-game earnings across 10+ abilities and build a loadout that fits your style.

Built to run smooth

Hundreds of balls on screen at 60 FPS, optimised so mid-range phones don't choke.

Plays offline

No connection needed. Leaderboards sync whenever you're back online.

Solar system chapters

Progress planet by planet, with difficulty and brick durability scaling as you go.

How to play

  • Slide and aim. Touch controls built for precise bounce angles, not fumbling.
  • Activate skills. Trigger abilities at the right moment to break open a stuck level.
  • Survive. Read the angles, kill the obstacles, and finish before the timer does.
