Frequently Asked Questions

What platforms are your games available on?

Brick Breaker: Rebuilt is available on Android via Google Play. Feywood is still in development, with platforms to be confirmed closer to release. A PC version is something we'd like to do, but nothing is promised yet.

Are your games free to play?

Yes. Brick Breaker: Rebuilt is free to download and play, and the full game is playable without spending anything. Progression is earned through normal play.

How do I report a bug or send feedback?

Either post it in our Discord or email celestails.studios@gmail.com. Discord is usually faster.

Including your phone model, Android version, and what you were doing when it broke makes a real difference to how quickly it gets fixed.

How can I become a tester?

We run small closed tests before each release, and we're usually looking for people. You don't need any technical background — honest feedback is the whole job. Testers get early builds and a credit on this site.

Apply on the contact page or ask in Discord.

Do I need an internet connection to play?

No. Brick Breaker: Rebuilt plays fully offline. A connection is only needed to sync leaderboard scores, which happens automatically next time you're online.

When is Feywood coming out?

There's no release date yet — it's still in active development and we'd rather ship it right than ship it fast. Screenshots, progress updates, and early test builds go to Discord first.

Who makes these games?

Celestails Studios is a small independent team. Brick Breaker: Rebuilt was developed by Enea Sebastian, with art by Nicola Andrei and a testing crew credited at the bottom of this page.

Can I make videos or stream your games?

Absolutely, and we'd love to see it. No permission needed — monetise it however you like. Send us a link and we may share it on our socials.
